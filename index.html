I am a Ph.D. candidate in mathematics at Stanford University, advised by Ravi Vakil.

I am interested in applications of algebraic geometry to matroids, particularly through wonderful compactifications. I am also broadly interested in combinatorics and algorithms.

Published

  • On the tangent bundle and the divisor theory of a general matroid

    International Mathematics Research Notices 2026, no. 17, rnag188.
    JournalarXiv

    Summary

    We construct a tangent K-class for every loopless matroid and develop a combinatorial divisor theory; in the realizable case, the class recovers the tangent bundle of the wonderful compactification.

Preprints

  • Kazhdan–Lusztig polynomials of matroids need not be unimodal

    With Shurui Liu · 2026 · arXiv

    Summary

    We construct representable matroids over every finite field whose Kazhdan–Lusztig polynomials are not unimodal, disproving the log-concavity and real-rootedness conjectures.

    The initial counterexample and the projective-deletion approach were produced by the AI system Rethlas; the appendix documents the AI-assisted discovery and the subsequent mathematical development.

  • Tangent classes for matroid building sets

    2026 · arXiv

    Summary

    We define tangent classes for arbitrary matroids with building sets and establish a matroidal Hirzebruch–Riemann–Roch identity and Chern-number inequalities.

  • Inequalities for Chow polynomials and Chern numbers of matroids

    With Wangyang Lin · 2026 · arXiv

    Summary

    We prove moment inequalities for normalized Chow coefficients, with consequences for flags of flats, roots of Chow polynomials, and matroidal Chern numbers.

AI for mathematics

Danus: Orchestrating Mathematical Reasoning Agents with Fact-Graph Memory

With Jihao Liu, Guoxiong Gao, Zeming Sun, Bin Wu, Shurui Liu, Jiedong Jiang, Haocheng Ju, Leheng Chen, Xiping Zhang, and Bin Dong.
Preprint, 2026 · arXiv

Summary

We introduce a mathematical reasoning system that coordinates multiple agents through a shared, verified fact graph, and evaluate it on six research-level case studies.

Related experiment documentation. Tangent classes of matroids and wonderful compactifications, with Shurui Liu and Guoxiong Gao (2026). A record of Danus-generated mathematical output, with editorial commentary. This report is not intended for journal publication.

Background on the Danus experiment

After I completed Tangent classes for matroid building sets , Shurui Liu and the Rethlas team tested their system on the same problem. It succeeded after they upgraded the system to Danus, independently reproducing the main results before my preprint was publicly available. This experiment led to my coauthorship of the Danus system paper and the accompanying record of its mathematical output.
